# Writing Boundaries

Paper Intelligence Workbench can organize local evidence for writing. It must
not become an automatic manuscript writer.

Allowed outputs:

- outlines;
- evidence matrices;
- claim banks;
- citation banks;
- paragraph plans;
- readiness reports;
- draft citation audits;
- revision checklists.

Disallowed outputs:

- fabricated claims;
- fabricated citations;
- fabricated quotes;
- fabricated paper summaries;
- polished final prose presented as user-authored text;
- cloud, LLM, or scraping-based draft interpretation.

When in doubt, emit an audit finding or checklist item rather than prose.

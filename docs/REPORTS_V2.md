# Reports v2

Reports are Markdown-first artifacts generated from local inputs.

## Stable Reports

- Inventory
- Reading status
- Papers by tag
- BibTeX audit
- Citation audit
- Evidence map
- Missing notes
- Weak claims
- Workspace health
- Dashboard

## Experimental Reports

- Evidence matrix
- Claim bank
- Citation bank
- Paragraph plan
- Writing packet
- Draft audit
- Manuscript QA
- Citation context table
- Claim traceability
- Sync plans and conflicts
- Backup/restore plans
- Migration plans
- Rule reports
- Local file audits
- Search index reports

## Interpretation Boundary

Reports organize local user-entered evidence. They do not certify scientific
truth, produce final prose, or validate that a citation truly supports a claim
beyond the user's own notes and transparent lexical checks.

## Historical Reports

Historical `v0_*`, `v1_*`, hostile-review, stress-test, and release-readiness
reports remain committed as audit history. They should not be treated as current
status unless they are explicitly regenerated for v2.0rc.

## Legacy Unversioned Reports

Unversioned reports such as `inventory.md`, `citation_audit.md`,
`evidence_map.md`, and `workspace_health.md` are example artifacts. They are
useful for demonstration but are not the v2.0rc release source of truth.
